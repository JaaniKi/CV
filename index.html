Projects

Software project · Tampere University · client: Solita

A web application that helps people living with coronary artery disease find reliable information and judge when to contact a professional. The chatbot retrieves information from Finnish and European cardiac care guidelines and generates answers with a language model. When a question can't be answered safely, the conversation is handed over to a nurse who replies in their own view. We built the prototype as a team in two-week sprints, and it was tested by nurses from Tampere Heart Hospital.

My contributions

  1. 01

    Message classifier

    Classifies patient messages as either safe for the chatbot to answer (RAG) or needing professional review.

  2. 02

    Emergency detection

    Built the first version of the module that detects urgent and crisis-related messages.

  3. 03

    Summary for professionals

    Flagged conversations get a structured summary: what the patient asked, medically relevant details and the reason for escalation. The RAG pipeline also drafts a reply the professional can review and approve.

  4. 04

    Current Care Guidelines

    Designed and built a feature that retrieves a matching evidence-based guideline from the knowledge base before escalating to a professional. An LLM picks the best match and extracts the most relevant sentences, and the answer links to the official source PDF.

  5. 05

    Chat titles

    AI-generated descriptive titles for conversations.

  6. 06

    Other

    Mostly backend work, some frontend, and bug fixes and smaller improvements throughout the project.
